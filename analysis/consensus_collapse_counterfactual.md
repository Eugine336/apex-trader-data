# Consensus-Collapse Counterfactual Analysis

_Generated by `analysis/run_counterfactual.py`. Re-run as more data lands._

## 0. Method & Coverage

A management snapshot with `scan_direction == NEUTRAL` marks a moment the directional consensus dissolved while a position was open. The **suppressed minority direction** is the side opposite the open position — the fast cluster that pulled panel agreement below the gate. We measure, from M5 OHLC, how far price moved in that suppressed direction afterward.

- Management snapshots parsed: **2248**
- NEUTRAL snapshots: **813**
- Distinct NEUTRAL windows (consecutive, per order): **76**
- NEUTRAL windows with M5 price coverage: **72** (94.7%)
- Hypothetical suppressed-scalp stop: **5 pips**

> **Coverage caveat:** most bundled M5 CSVs end **2026-06-12 23:55** (a few, e.g. BTCUSD, reach mid June 13), while the decision journal spans **June 11–15**. NEUTRAL windows on June 13–15 therefore have no price data and are excluded from excursion math. Notably, **all 335 BTCUSD NEUTRAL snapshots fall on June 13–15**, so the covered window set is FX/JPY-dominated. All expectancy numbers below are computed on the covered subset only.

> **Unit caveat:** 'pips' are FX/JPY pips. Crypto, metals, energy and index instruments use native-unit conversions (see `pip_multiplier`) and are not directly comparable; they are near-absent from the covered subset (1 crypto window), so the aggregate reflects FX behaviour.

## 7. Verdict

**No clear edge on covered data.** The suppressed direction's 30-min expectancy is **+1.9 pips** (≈+0.37R), only **-0.4 pips** vs the aligned-scan base rate — within noise. The NEUTRAL collapse is mostly suppressing chop, not systematically-profitable reversals, on this sample.

- Double-miss events (holding a loser while the suppressed side ran ≥5p): **17** (23.6% of covered windows).

> **Caveats:** (1) MFE/MAE use M5 bars — intrabar path is approximated; a 5-pip stop and target could both be touched in one bar. (2) Coverage is limited to June 11–12; the full sample needs June 13–15 M5 data. (3) Expectancy ignores spread/slippage and the existing reversal size-down and falling-knife guards.
